## Getting help

Need help with Vinyl Mosaic? Email **vinylmosaic@icloud.com** and we'll do our best to help.

## Common questions

- **The wall is empty.** Vinyl Mosaic shows the albums in your Apple Music library. Add albums
  in the Music app (or turn on Sync Library), and make sure at least one collection is
  switched on.
- **No music plays.** Playing songs needs an Apple Music subscription.
- **Collections don't match between my Apple TV and iPhone.** Both must be signed into the
  same Apple ID with iCloud turned on; changes sync within a minute or so.
- **Play on HomePod or Sonos.** Use the AirPlay button on the iPhone, or "Play on speakers"
  in the Apple TV menu (click the remote while the wall is showing). Sonos speakers need
  AirPlay 2.
- **OLED TVs.** The wall drifts slightly and moves its captions to avoid burn-in, and
  Settings → "Turn off the wall" stops it after a set time.

## Reporting a problem

Please include:

- Your device (e.g. Apple TV 4K, iPhone Duo) and its software version
- Vinyl Mosaic's version and code (Settings → About)
- What you were doing, what happened, and what you expected
